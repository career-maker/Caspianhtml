Royal Beluga & Baerii Caviar

Harvested from mature sturgeons reared in pristine aquatic ecosystems adhering to historic Azerbaijani traditions. Hand-selected for large firm beads (3.0mm to 3.4mm) with rich anthracite marbling, delicate buttery hazelnut nuances, and a silkily clean ocean finish.

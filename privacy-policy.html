Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026 • Caspian & Sun Food Trading LLC

At Caspian & Sun Food Trading LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”), we hold the privacy and trust of our wholesale clients, institutional procurement partners, and website visitors with the utmost gravity. This Privacy Policy clarifies how personal and commercial data is gathered, processed, and safeguarded across all digital touchpoints and corporate transactions.

1. Information We Collect

When engaging with our digital platforms or commercial desks, we may collect the following classifications of information:

  • Commercial & Contact Identifiers: Name, professional title, corporate affiliation, business email address, direct phone/WhatsApp number, and country of operation.
  • Transactional & Fulfillment Data: Port of delivery, order quotas, shipping documentation, customs clearance parameters, and billing information necessary to execute international trade contracts.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized IP addresses, browser specifications, operating system versions, and interaction metrics gathered via security logs to optimize user experience and prevent denial-of-service disruptions.

2. How We Utilize Your Data

Every piece of data collected is processed strictly under legitimate commercial interests and statutory compliance:

  • To formulate customized price quotations, volume allocations, and product specification dossiers.
  • To manage freight logistics, cold-chain temperature telemetry, customs manifests, and veterinary health inspections.
  • To administer corporate account correspondence, payment settlement, and trade contract verifications.
  • To dispatch critical seasonal market notices, quota availability updates, or regulatory advisories to registered wholesale partners (with immediate opt-out rights).

3. International Data Transfers & Supply Chain Partners

Operating an international seafood trade network spanning the United Arab Emirates, Turkey, and global transit hubs requires seamless data synchronization. Your information may be transmitted to verified logistics brokers, maritime shipping carriers, and customs agencies solely for the purpose of executing contracted product shipments.

All third-party logistics and software partners are bound by confidentiality covenants and required to demonstrate technical and organizational measures aligned with international data standards (including GDPR and UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 regarding Personal Data Protection).

4. Data Retention & Security Protocols

We implement multi-layered physical, technical, and managerial safeguards—including TLS encryption for web data transmission and restricted access protocols for commercial customer databases. Commercial records and invoices are maintained for statutory periods mandated by commercial trade law and tax authorities, after which they are securely expunged.

5. Cookies & Digital Tracking

Our website utilizes strictly essential session cookies to ensure proper page rendering, language retention, and secure form transmission. We do not sell, rent, or trade user behavioral data to third-party ad exchanges or tracking conglomerates.

6. Your Statutory Rights

Under prevailing data protection legislation, you are entitled to:

  • Request confirmation and inspection of any personal information we hold concerning your account.
  • Request rectification of erroneous or outdated corporate records.
  • Request erasure of your non-statutory commercial records (“Right to be Forgotten”), subject to outstanding contractual or fiscal obligations.
  • Revoke consent for commercial trade communications at any time.

Our Story

WHO WE ARE

CASPIAN & SUN FOOD TRADING is an international food trading and distribution company with operations in Türkiye and the United Arab Emirates, specializing in the import, export, sourcing, sales, and distribution of premium food products for the HORECA sector (Hotels, Restaurants, Catering), luxury hospitality businesses, wholesalers, distributors, and retail markets.

Our story began in 1981 in Azerbaijan as a family-owned business founded on integrity, trust, and a passion for supplying quality food products. Through decades of continuous growth, we expanded our operations into Türkiye and the United Arab Emirates, building strong international partnerships and an extensive global sourcing network.

Today, with our headquarters in Istanbul, Türkiye, and expanding operations in Dubai, UAE, we connect world-class food manufacturers with businesses throughout the Middle East and international markets.

Where We Are Headed

OUR VISION

To become one of the leading international food trading companies in Türkiye and the United Arab Emirates by connecting premium food manufacturers with luxury hotels, restaurants, catering companies, wholesalers, retailers, and foodservice distributors through reliable sourcing, competitive pricing, and exceptional customer service.

What We Do

OUR MISSION

To supply premium-quality food products sourced from trusted manufacturers around the world while providing reliable sourcing solutions, competitive pricing, efficient logistics, and exceptional customer service.

We are committed to building long-term partnerships based on trust, integrity, transparency, and mutual success while creating lasting value for our customers, suppliers, and business partners.

Our Strategic Goal

A BRIDGE BETWEEN GLOBAL PRODUCERS AND MARKETS

Our primary goal is to establish Caspian & Sun Food Trading as a leading international sourcing, importing, and food supply company in the UAE and Türkiye, creating a strong bridge between global food producers and the growing hospitality, foodservice, wholesale, and retail markets in both countries.

We aim to develop a trusted global supplier network, expand our international product portfolio, and provide customers with access to premium products from the world's leading food-producing regions through a reliable and professionally managed supply chain.

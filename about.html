WHO WE ARE

Our story began in 1981 with a caviar farm on the shores of the Caspian Sea in Azerbaijan. What started as a small family initiative soon grew into a passion-driven journey, combining traditional expertise with modern innovation in seafood production. Today, with our headquarters in Istanbul, we proudly deliver premium seafood and gourmet delicacies to customers across the globe. Every product reflects decades of experience, care, and a deep respect for the oceans. In 2015, under the vision and leadership of Vurghun Sevdimaliyev, our company established its corporate structure in Turkey. With a strong focus on quality and sustainability, we quickly gained nationwide recognition through the Sun Caspian Seafood brands, becoming a trusted name in the seafood industry. Our journey continues as we strive to bring the finest flavors of the Caspian to dining tables worldwide, always committed to excellence, innovation, and responsible practices.

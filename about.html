Heritage & Excellence

A Legacy of Pure Ocean Delicacies & Global Trade

From pioneering sturgeon aquaculture on the Caspian Sea in 1981 to supplying five-star hotels and premium markets worldwide, we curate exceptional gastronomic treasures with uncompromising integrity.

Established 1981

Beyond the Ocean

Rooted in Tradition, Driven by International Innovation

Our story began in 1981 with a dedicated sturgeon caviar farm on the pristine shores of the Caspian Sea in Azerbaijan. What began as an artisanal family craft evolved into a lifelong devotion to sustainable aquaculture, gastronomic mastery, and responsible ocean stewardship.

In 2015, under the vision and leadership of Vurghun Sevdimaliyev, the company reinforced its global corporate operations, establishing key strategic hubs in Istanbul and Dubai. Through our distinguished Sun Caspian Seafood brand, we established new benchmarks for cold-chain precision, freshness, and culinary pedigree.

Who We Are

Global Purveyors of Refined Taste

We are seasoned food specialists, marine biologists, culinary partners, and logistics experts unified by a singular purpose: connecting distinguished chefs, hotel operators, and gourmet retailers with the finest seafood and butchery products available.

Our Middle East headquarters in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, positions Caspian & Sun Food Trading LLC as the premier gateway for rapid temperature-controlled distribution across the GCC, Europe, and Asia.

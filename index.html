About

About
Caspian

CASPIAN & SUN FOOD TRADING is an international food trading and distribution company with operations in Türkiye and the United Arab Emirates, specializing in the import, export, sourcing, sales, and distribution of premium food products for the HORECA sector (Hotels, Restaurants, Catering), luxury hospitality businesses, wholesalers, distributors, and retail markets.

Our story began in 1981 in Azerbaijan as a family-owned business founded on integrity, trust, and a passion for supplying quality food products. Through decades of continuous growth, we expanded our operations into Türkiye and the United Arab Emirates, building strong international partnerships and an extensive global sourcing network.
